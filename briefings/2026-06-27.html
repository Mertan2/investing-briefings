  • MU faded its blowout. The stock fell −6.69% to $1,132.33, giving back roughly half the +17% post-earnings pop. The "no chase into the spike" discipline was vindicated a second time — anyone who chased the ~$1,233 intraday peak is now underwater. The super-cycle thesis is intact (arguably stronger), but the easy money is gone.
  • The Mag7 drag is SPREADING — the down-branch tell. The Nasdaq fell −0.24% to 25,297.62, its fifth consecutive losing session; Apple −6.12% (its worst single day in over a year, ~$265B erased) and Microsoft −3.5%, both blaming surging memory/RAM costs for price hikes ("RAMageddon"). The S&P closed −0.05% to 7,354.02 (still ~flat) and the Dow −0.09%, but breadth deteriorated.
  • The Fed is CONFIRMED HAWKISH. Under Chair Kevin Warsh, the June dot plot flipped to project a 2026 HIKE (17 of 18 officials see inflation risks to the upside); September-hike odds sit ~62–63%, December ~80%; the 10-year is 4.38%. This directly refutes the 26 June premise that "the market absorbed hot PCE / rate fear is lifting" — the premise the ASML upgrade rested on.

But two offsets keep this from being a clean down-branch: the SOX actually rose +3.2% Friday on memory leadership (MU led early before fading), and Brent crashed to ~$72.95 (−3.07% on the day, ~−10% on the week, the lowest since 27 Feb) on US–Iran peace progress — a genuine disinflationary counterweight to the hawkish Fed and a consumer tailwind. Gold failed again (~$3,994), validating the 26 June NEM swap-out.

The key new frame — the "RAMageddon" double-edge: the AI-memory super-cycle that powered MU's blowout is now causing downstream cost inflation that hits the Mag7 device makers — Apple and Microsoft both raising prices on memory costs. It is bullish for memory pricing power (MU/WDC/SNDK) but bearish for mega-cap demand and margins — which is exactly why the Mag7 drag spreads even as the SOX holds. The disciplined response: trim the most rate-exposed name and lean on the rate-insensitive ballast.

  • LLY is the standout. +6.15% Friday, named the "clear winner" of the ADA weight-loss conference — the defensive anchor working exactly as designed into a hawkish/risk-off tilt.
  • No whipsaw elsewhere. TSM stays High (lower-multiple logic core, far less rate-exposed than ASML), the power/uranium sleeve (TLN/CEG/CCJ) is more valuable now the hawkish Fed is confirmed, and VRT — the 26 June swap-in — fell through its add zone (~$303–315) but the thesis is intact (Bernstein Buy, Roth $355).
