How to read this page — the UI, stated explicitly

Nothing on this page relies on you inferring meaning from a colour. Every element is defined here:

  • Conviction pills. High = full intended position size, thesis confirmed by something outside the price. Med = thesis alive but a stated gate has failed, so the position is held at reduced size and is not added to. A row with no change-tag changed nothing today.
  • Change tags. DOWNGRADE UPGRADE NEW MISS mark what changed since the previous briefing. MISS is new today: it means a price zone this book pre-committed to expired unfilled and is being scored as a failure rather than quietly re-quoted.
  • Gates. Every position carries one numeric condition that must print on a closing basis before its status can move. A gate is not an opinion — it fires or it does not. The gate proximity meter below is new: it shows how close each gate came on Monday, because "nearly fired" is the entire story of this session and a table of unchanged statuses would hide it.
  • The two sections that are about you personally. Your portfolio scores the 11 positions you actually hold and gives one action per line — hold / no-add / trim-to-core / stop-broken. The watch-list is the queue: names not in the ten targets, each carrying the specific price or event that would promote it. Nothing enters the ten targets without first appearing on the watch-list with a gate attached — that rule is why CAT is queued today rather than swapped in this morning.
  • The ten targets are research ideas, deliberately capped at ten so adding one requires removing one. They are not your holdings. They overlap only where a holding is also the best current expression of a live thesis — today MU and NVDA.
  • Colour. Green = confirmed positive. Red = confirmed negative. Amber = unresolved, pending, or a figure this book could not verify. Amber never means "mildly bad"; it means "not settled."
