  • Pre-commitments — rules written before the event they govern. The pre-commitment section exists so that tomorrow's edition cannot quietly invent a more comfortable rule. Deadlines set there are dates, not intentions.
  • Your portfolio vs. the watch-list — two different things, deliberately kept apart.   • Weekend editions are different, and say so. There are no new closes on a Saturday. This edition scores Friday and re-states the rules. It fires no gates — see pre-commitment 4.
