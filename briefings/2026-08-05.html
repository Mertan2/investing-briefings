How to read this page — the UI, stated explicitly

Nothing on this page relies on you inferring meaning from a colour. Every element is defined here:

  • Conviction pills. High = full intended position size, thesis confirmed by something outside the price. Med = thesis alive but a stated gate has failed, so the position is held at reduced size and is not added to. A row with no change-tag changed nothing today.
  • Change tags. UPGRADE DOWNGRADE NEW MISS GATE FIRED mark what changed since the previous briefing. Today there is exactly one: MU. GATE FIRED is new on this page — it means a written numeric condition printed and the status moved mechanically, with no judgement applied.
  • Gates. Every position carries one numeric condition that must print on a closing basis before its status can move. A gate is not an opinion — it fires or it does not. The gate proximity meter below shows exactly how each one resolved on Tuesday. Read it first. It is the entire scorecard in eight bars, and it contains the two numbers that define this edition: MU's +$12.67 through the line and VRT's −$0.07 short of it.
  • Pre-commitments. The pre-commitment section is where rules are written before the events that test them, so they cannot be quietly rewritten afterwards. Today it contains three, including the XOM rule that arguably fired and is being converted into something harder-edged rather than either executed or ignored — with the reasoning shown, not summarised.
  • The two sections that are about you personally. Your portfolio scores the 11 positions you actually hold and gives one action per line — hold / no-add / trim-to-core / stop-broken / not-verified. The watch-list is the queue: names not in the ten targets, each carrying the specific price or event that would promote it. Nothing enters the ten targets without first appearing on the watch-list with a gate attached.
  • The ten targets are research ideas, deliberately capped at ten so adding one requires removing one. They are not your holdings. They overlap only where a holding is also the best current expression of a live thesis — today MU and NVDA.
  • Colour. Green = confirmed positive. Red = confirmed negative. Amber = unresolved, pending, or a figure this book could not verify. Amber never means "mildly bad"; it means "not settled."
