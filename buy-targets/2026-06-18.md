**Why it matters:** higher-for-longer — and now a possible hike — is the single biggest macro headwind to a *long-duration, high-multiple AI/semis book*. Rising yields + a strong dollar compress exactly the multiples this book tilts to. It is the second straight headwind (16 Jun sell-the-news, 17 Jun rate shock) — but a **discount-rate repricing, not a thesis break.**

**Net: 10 KEEP, conviction re-sorted, no ticker swap.** The disciplined response to a one-day macro-rate shock is *not* to break theses or chase new high-multiple risk — it is to hold the structural/defensive names, lean on the **dated mechanical catalysts that don't care about the Fed** (MRVL S&P 500 inclusion 22 Jun = forced passive buying; MU print 24 Jun), and downgrade the most rate-exposed name.
