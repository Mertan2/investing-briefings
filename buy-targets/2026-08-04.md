## The one-paragraph version

Monday was the session this book had been positioned for and, on the aggregate, positioned correctly — and it still produced a losing scorecard on the book's own terms. A book full of conditions that are all *nearly* met is a book that captured the direction and none of the permissions. That is the honest read of the day, and it is why there are no upgrades below.
