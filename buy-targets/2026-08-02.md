## What actually changed while the market was shut

1. **The single biggest seller was a fund being liquidated, and it has now been removed.** Citadel acquired the majority of Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness hedge fund's roughly **$16 billion public-equity portfolio** in a privately negotiated block deal, outbidding Millennium and Jane Street, explicitly pre-empting a disorderly open-market fire sale of an AI-heavy book. Reaction: **Samsung +27%**, **SK Hynix +30%**, **KOSPI +18%**, the Roundhill Memory ETF (`DRAM`) off its August low of **$44.40** to about **$49.75–$50.30**. *Prices that move that far on the removal of a seller were not being set by fundamentals in the first place.* This reframes Saturday's downgrade; it does not reverse it.
2. **SanDisk was sold 12–14% on a DRAM story, and SanDisk does not make DRAM.** CXMT is a DRAM manufacturer with **no current NAND presence**; SanDisk is pure NAND and was among the hardest hit. That is a market selling a ticker for its ETF membership. Meanwhile **Goldman raised SanDisk to $2,200 from $1,200**, modelling NAND pricing **+75% to +100%**, with long-term-agreement floors near **$0.29/GB** — broadly in line with current ASPs, so the contracted floor is not a discount to spot. SanDisk's **investor day is 13 August**, eight days after the print.
3. **The quantified CXMT threat is much smaller than the narrative one.** **Mizuho**: CXMT DRAM bit-supply growth **+13% in 2027** vs a **21%** industry; 2026–2028 CAGR **24%**, in line — *"no reckless supply expansion to try and take share with aggressive pricing."* Global DRAM share ~**7.7%** in Q4. And the constraint is physical: **Applied Materials and Lam Research suspended tool servicing on CXMT's installed base in March 2026**, much of it around a decade old. CXMT's Shanghai debut closed ~**466%** above the offer price and it swung from a ¥2.83B operating loss to a **>¥35B operating profit** — the company is real; the near-term supply threat is smaller than the move implied.
4. **The counter-argument, stated fairly: the leverage is not cleaned out.** **Academy Securities**: taking the seller out helped, but *"there is so much money in this space that is leveraged … we're going to start seeing some selling pressure again in the next two weeks."* Korean margin calls exceeded **₩61bn (~$42M)** on Thursday; Samsung and SK Hynix are **26.4%** and **22.7%** of the DRAM ETF, so Korean leverage transmits straight into the US names.
5. **OPEC+ met today, Sunday 2 August.** The eight core producers (Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, UAE, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria, Oman) convened to approve **+188,000 bpd** for September — a fourth identical monthly step that **completes** the unwind of the 2023 voluntary cuts — with delegates signalling a likely **pause from October 2026 through January 2027**, not yet formally adopted. The number that matters more: the group was pumping roughly **7.5M bpd below its collective target** in June. **Quotas are not barrels.**
